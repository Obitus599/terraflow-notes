Ras Al Khor car dealers

12 dealers in two clusters. Every one runs the same DubiCars website template, so the pitch is the same at every door: buyers can't WhatsApp you from your own site. Five of them show no cars at all.

At the door: open their page, show finding 1 on the iPad, then hand them their own site on your phone and let them look for the WhatsApp button. Offer: we fix it from AED 4,000, fixed price first. Before you leave: tap "Send them the page" so the link lands in their WhatsApp.
Map pins search by name and showroom number. Showroom numbers come from each dealer's own contact page, so trust the number on the door over the pin.
