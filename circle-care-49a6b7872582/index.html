1 Fix first

Circa AI fails every question

What it meansCirca is in your main menu, so parents find it. I asked it three questions, including one it suggests itself, and got the same error each time.
The fixThe chat box loads, then every answer comes back as an error. Whoever built it should start with the account behind it. A lapsed AI subscription is a common cause. After that, ask it one question a week.
Likely under a day

2 Fix first

"Book in 60 seconds" never asks when

What it meansA parent fills it in at 9pm and has no idea if it's tomorrow or next week. So they message as well, or call another clinic. Your site carries Google Ads tracking, so some of these visitors are paid for.
The fixAdd a day and a morning or afternoon choice to the form. Your team then calls back with a slot that already suits them. Full live booking can come later.
A day or two

3 Worth doing

"Book with Dr. Rania" forgets Dr. Rania

What it meansThe parent picked her by name. Your team gets a request with no doctor on it and has to ask again.
The fixThe form has no place for a doctor. Each doctor's button should pass their name in and show it at the top.
An hour or two

4 Worth doing

It says "Open now" at 11:30pm

↓
What it meansThe badge and the three location cards say Open all day, every day. A parent who trusts it on a Sunday finds the door shut.
The fixWork it out from your real hours, which are already on the page.
About an hour
