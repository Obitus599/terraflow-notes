Losing you bookings

Turns people away

Your FAQ tells people they need a licence

I have never driven a Jet Ski, can I take part in the trip? Yes, our Popeye instructors are here to take care of you as well as explain to you how to use the Jet Ski. Book your ride here!
Do I need a boat or Jetski license to drive the Jetski? Yes, our Popeye instructors are here to take care of you as well as explain to you how to use the Jet Ski. Book your ride here!

Both answers are the same sentence, word for word.

Somebody pasted the first answer under the second question. Read the second one on its own, which is how a nervous tourist reads it, and it says yes, you need a licence. They do not read the rest. They close the tab and book with someone else.

This is the one question a first timer asks before handing over a card, and it is currently answered wrong on your homepage.

Seven of your twelve activities ask for a day and a time. The other five just say Add to cart. Someone pays for a flyboard session and you have no idea when they are turning up, so your staff find out when they walk onto the jetty.

The clearest sign this is a settings mistake and not a decision: your Jet Car 20 minute asks for a date and your Jet Car 30 minute does not. Same machine, same jetty, two different behaviours.

Worth tidying

Unproven claim

Your homepage shows 4.9 and ten thousand positive reviews.
