Four findings. Each one sits between a collector and an email.

01 For Edward Horswell

Your Enquire button only works for people with a mail app set up

You said the site's job is enquiries about a specific piece, so that is the only thing I looked at. I went through sladmore.com on a phone the way a collector would: find a work, open it, try to ask about it. The button that does the asking is an email link, with nothing behind it. The part you would expect to be hardest, telling you which piece someone means, you already do well.

02 Fix today

Costs you enquiries

Enquire opens an email, and there is no form to fall back on

What Enquire doesOpens mail
If that failsNothing

Every artwork page has one Enquire button, and it is a mailto link. Tap it and the device tries to open its default mail app with a message to contact@sladmore.com already written. If the visitor has that app set up, it works. If they read email in a browser, Gmail or Outlook on the web, they get a setup screen or nothing at all. There is no second way to ask sitting next to the button.

I looked for one. The only form anywhere on the site is the newsletter signup. The phone number is on every artwork page, but at the very bottom, under two dozen other works.

You also can't see this from inside the gallery. When someone taps Enquire and gives up, nothing records it. At best your analytics log the tap. They can't log the email that never got sent, so a working button and a broken one look the same in your numbers.

03 Costing you enquiries

Slow to reach the button

One sculpture page downloads 54 MB on a phone

  • Large Stretching Panther54.2 MB
  • Mighty Horse, 202323.2 MB
  • Mighty Horse, main photo5.3 MB
  • Images set to load later0

The Panther page shows the Panther, then a strip of Mark Coreth's other work. That strip is 102 more pieces, and the page downloads every one of their photographs the moment it opens, at full size, whether anyone scrolls to them or not. On a good 4G signal that is around twenty seconds of downloading for one bronze, and much longer in a gallery basement or on hotel wifi.

Mighty Horse is lighter at 23 MB, but its main photograph is a 5.3 MB PNG displayed on a phone at about 330 pixels wide. PNG is built for logos and screenshots. For a photograph it stores far more than anyone can see.

This is fixable without touching how anything looks. Tell the strip of other works to load as people scroll to it, and save the photographs as WebP or a sensibly sized JPEG.

04 Worth tidying

You publish separate addresses for Antique, Modern, Contemporary and Monumental. Every Enquire button I tried sends to contact@ instead. If someone reads the general inbox and forwards on, that works. It is still one extra hop between a collector and the person who knows the cast.

No sculpture on the first screen

The Artworks page opens on a red banner and a filter

On a phone, the whole first screen of Artworks is the page title and the filter boxes. The first sculpture is below it. With 556 works across 47 pages of twelve, the filter matters, but the first thing a collector sees on a sculpture gallery's site should be sculpture.

05 What is already working

When the email does open, it tells you exactly which piece

The Enquire link fills in the subject line with the title, the date and the artist: "Enquiry about Mighty Horse, 2023, Nic Fiddian Green". Nobody has to ask which one they meant, and that's the hard part done.

The pages themselves are what a serious buyer wants to read. Dimensions come in inches and centimetres, the edition is stated, and on the older works the foundry and patina are written out too. The photography is excellent. I'd leave all of that alone and fix the one step between wanting a piece and asking about it.

06 The order I would do them in

  1. Put a short form next to EnquireName, email, a phone number if they want to give one, and the piece filled in the way the email already does it. Keep the email and phone beside it for people who prefer them.
  2. Load the other works as people scrollOne setting on the image strip. It cuts the Panther page to a fraction of its 54 MB.
  3. Resave the large photographsStarting with anything saved as PNG. Same pictures, a fraction of the weight.
  4. Route enquiries by departmentThe site already knows each piece's category, so the form can send a Bugatti to Modern.
  5. Show sculpture on the first screen of ArtworksShorter banner, or the first row of works above the filter.
