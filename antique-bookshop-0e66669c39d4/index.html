1 Fix first

On a phone, every word is tiny

What it meansYour page is built nearly three phone screens wide, so the phone shrinks all of it to fit. People have to pinch and zoom to read your hours. Most give up.
The fixTell the page how wide a phone is, and let the row of book photos wrap onto two lines. Nothing else changes.
